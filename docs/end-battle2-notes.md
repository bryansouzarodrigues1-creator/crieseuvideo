# End battle, part 2: direction revision

The canonical imported Code 3D program contains twelve ten-second scenes and 38 directed shots. The camera cuts follow dialogue, decisions, contact and reactions. Several captions are now short character exchanges. Dialogue remains subtitled; no recorded voices are generated.

The six voxel actors keep their established design. Eye direction, individual blinks, nods, a pointing gesture, torso preparation and staggered reactions add intentions to the movement. The rescue is shown from a side angle to retain both faces and the shared hand target. Three team impacts replace the static hold in the middle of the battle, with alternating blows, a combined hit and dragon recoil.

The leader moves around to the protected side of the cover during the breath attack and reaches the front of the final formation. Footsteps are regenerated from the same travel-based gait, and the new impacts have sound cues. The original MIT notice remains in the source; no game model or texture is downloaded.

The existing studio import, opaque WebGL sandbox and native export button remain the rendering route. Browser preview retains frames of every directed shot plus a native action clip; full export uses four groups of three clips. Offline checks test reach, finite transforms, foot targets and scenery occlusion, and supplement the actual browser render. No model request, renderer replacement or production deployment is part of this fixture.
